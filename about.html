Company History

From Mathura roots to a pan-India industrial brand

Founded by industry specialists in 1993, Mukesh Niwar Industries has built a reputation for dependable quality, timely deliveries, and strong customer partnerships. We serve a wide range of sectors including bag manufacturing, apparel, home textiles, furniture, construction and safety gear. Our range includes Red Namo Niwar, cotton niwar, braided ropes, curtain tapes, reflective tapes and yarns used by major brands and traders across India.

Our mission is simple: build lasting relationships through consistent quality, reliable delivery, and affordable innovation. Every order is handled with the same attention to detail and commitment to strength.
